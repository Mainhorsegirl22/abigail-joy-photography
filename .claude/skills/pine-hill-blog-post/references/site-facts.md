- Small family breeder, Garland, Penobscot County, Maine. About an hour north of Bangor, two hours from Portland. 40+ acres.
- Phone 207-703-8043. Email pinehillgermanshepherds@gmail.com.
- One breeding female: Freda von Stephanitz, dark sable, born 1 July 2023, AKC DN76717801. PennHIP hips, OFA elbows, heart, eyes normal, genetics clear. Trained in scent detection and search and rescue. Swims, hikes, does bite work and obedience, then sleeps on the kitchen floor.
- Sire of the current litter: Ledger Bei Mackenzie von Franzosisches Haus, called Rangeley, Czech lines, owned by Mackenzie German Shepherds. OFA Good hips, Normal elbows, heart echo normal, CAER eyes normal, Embark clear, DM clear.
- Puppies raised in the house on Puppy Culture: early neurological stimulation days 3 to 16, sound and surface work as eyes and ears open, early scent introduction, crate and car exposure, first clicker behaviours before eight weeks. Temperament tested at seven weeks. Matched to families on temperament, never by photo. Puppies go home at eight weeks.
- Price $3,000, $500 non-refundable deposit applied to the price, balance at pickup. Limited AKC registration. Written 24 month hip and elbow guarantee, 3 day vet check window.
- K9 Scholars: optional five week extra training program after eight weeks.
- Owner's voice sample (her own edit): "As the breeder I have spent every single moment of the 8 weeks old with our puppies, watching working and observing them. Most unexperienced dog owners won't be able to determine a puppies temperament in just a 30 minute window of time."
