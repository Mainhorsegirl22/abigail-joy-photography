welcome to pine hill

Family Raised Working-Line German Shepherds in Northern Maine

We’re a small family business raising working-line German Shepherds on 40 acres of rural countryside in the backwoods of New England. We specialize in producing high-quality German Shepherds known for their exceptional personalities, working drive, scent detection abilities, and balanced temperaments.

With only one breeding female, Freda — our beloved family member — we have the unique opportunity to carefully plan each litter, dedicating countless hours to observing and working with our puppies. Raised within our home and surrounded by our family, they receive plenty of love and care from day one.

We Believe in Going the Extra Mile

All of our dogs are proudly registered with the AKC — but registration is only a starting point. To ensure our puppies grow up healthy and happy, we conduct comprehensive genetic and health screenings through reputable veterinarians.

Full OFA Evaluations

k9 scholars

Taking Puppy Training to the Next Level

Puppies possess a remarkable ability to learn. Given a structured environment tailored to their rapidly developing brains, their potential knows no bounds. During their five-week comprehensive training program, they learn advanced manners and are introduced to new situations:

  • Advanced manners — sit, stay, come, leash
  • Crate training
  • Manding — automatic sit
  • Food manners
  • Advanced socialization
  • Scent detection exercises
  • Confidence building
  • Manners in public
