Our Story

We're a Small Family Kennel

Welcome to Pine Hill German Shepherds! We're a small, family-operated breeder nestled in the serene backwoods of northern Maine. Our main breeding female, Freda, is a beloved part of our home and family — and with only one breeding female, we're able to raise every litter within our own household.

Our mission is to raise confident, healthy German Shepherd puppies from fully health-tested parents renowned for their intelligence, working drive, and unwavering loyalty.

Our Dogs

Why Working-Line German Shepherds?

Health Tested

Every parent is fully health tested — OFA hips, elbows, eyes and heart, plus comprehensive genetic panel screening.

Working Drive & Intelligence

Bred from proven working lines: confident, intelligent, and versatile enough to thrive in any environment.

Early Puppy Development

Every puppy goes through Puppy Culture along with ESI and early neurological stimulation from day one.

Raised, Not Just Bred

With each litter, we reserve two spots for puppies to stay with us for advanced obedience and training. These puppies live in our home, working through our positive-reinforcement program that builds confident, well-behaved companions ready to succeed anywhere.
